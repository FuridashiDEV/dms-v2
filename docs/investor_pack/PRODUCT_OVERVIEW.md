# Product Overview

DocFlow helps teams store, find, relate and verify business documents.

## Core workflows

- Upload a document and preserve the file.
- Create a version record and hash metadata.
- Classify by department, folder, type, status and retention.
- Search by text, entities and semantic meaning.
- View why a result was found.
- Open related documents such as contract, appendix, act, invoice and addendum.
- Export a human-readable evidence report.
- Exchange documents with a counterparty through controlled secure links.

## User roles

- Admin: organization setup, user management, dashboards, billing/cost/security views.
- Employee: document upload, search and access based on department and explicit grants.
- External counterparty: token-based portal actions for specific exchanges only.

## Limitations

- No e-signature/ЭЦП.
- No full public API developer portal.
- No payment gateway.
- No claim of legal certification.
- AI suggestions require human review before official document fields are changed.
