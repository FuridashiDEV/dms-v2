# DocFlow One-Pager

DocFlow is an AI-assisted enterprise document flow platform for controlled pilots in organizations that need searchable archives, document access control, auditability, and safe AI processing.

## Problem

Business documents are often stored in folders, email chains and isolated systems. Teams lose time finding contracts, acts, invoices, appendices and evidence around decisions. AI search is useful only if it respects permissions, explains why results are found, and does not expose sensitive data.

## Product

DocFlow provides:

- Document archive with versions, statuses, departments and roles.
- Organization isolation and object-level access.
- Audit events and usage tracking.
- Semantic and hybrid search with entity extraction, aliases and explanations.
- Related document graph for contract chains.
- Evidence package/report for internal review.
- B2B exchange foundation with secure external links.
- AI/OCR processing foundation with cost-aware policies.

## Current status

Pilot-ready enterprise MVP for investor demo and controlled first pilots. The product is not positioned as SOC2/ISO-certified, legally binding e-signature, or fully autonomous AI agent system.

## Near-term commercial motion

Controlled pilots, LOI discussions, pilot proposals, security review, and customer validation.
