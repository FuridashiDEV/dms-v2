# Investor FAQ

## Is DocFlow production-ready?

It is pilot-ready for controlled enterprise MVP deployments. Full production hardening depends on pilot environment, SLA, legal review and customer requirements.

## Does it use AI?

Yes. AI is used for search, extraction suggestions and processing foundations. Official fields are not changed without human confirmation.

## Does it guarantee 99-100% search accuracy?

No. The product measures search quality through benchmark commands and reports limitations honestly.

## Is it certified for SOC2/ISO?

No certification is claimed.

## Is there e-signature?

No. Evidence report is not an e-signature or legal certification feature.

## Are there real customers?

The repository should not claim signed customers unless separately confirmed. Materials should use "pilot discussions" or "preliminary interest" where accurate.
