# Karen Airapetyants: Founder Call Brief

## Objective

Establish investor fit and earn a second conversation. Do not try to close legal terms on the first call.

## Suggested 20-minute flow

1. 2 minutes: why the document workflow problem is expensive and risky.
2. 5 minutes: show the demo chain and permission-aware search.
3. 4 minutes: explain the controlled-pilot wedge and target buyer.
4. 4 minutes: current readiness, what is proven in the product and what remains a pilot hypothesis.
5. 3 minutes: intended use of the USD 100k-150k round.
6. 2 minutes: ask for the investor's fit, feedback and next step.

## Good answers

### What is built?

Describe the implemented workflows: documents, versions, permissions, audit, hybrid search, explanations, document relations, evidence reports, B2B exchange foundation and controlled processing. Offer a live demo instead of a feature dump.

### What is not yet proven?

Repeat the limitations: production traction, customer-specific benchmark data, formal legal/commercial package and full enterprise deployment commitments will be validated through pilots.

### How will capital be used?

Controlled pilots, reliability, validated search-quality improvement, onboarding and legal/operational formation. Do not promise a specific revenue figure without a validated model.

### Why this valuation/dilution range?

"We are targeting a USD 100k-150k pre-seed round with an indicative 10-15% dilution range. We are open to discussing a suitable instrument and terms with the right investor after legal formation."

## Escalate before answering

- Equity, valuation cap, discount, SAFE, convertible note or shareholder rights.
- Jurisdiction, incorporation date, tax treatment or IP assignment.
- Customer names, data access, security incident history or non-public metrics.
- Any request for source code, credentials, full audit logs or real documents.
