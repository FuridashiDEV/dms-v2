# DocFlow Investor Targets: First Cohort

This is a research-backed starting cohort, not a claim that these organisations are actively fundraising, will invest, or are an endorsement. Use each organisation's official route and re-check current application requirements before contact.

## Personalisation fields before outreach

- Investor's documented stage focus.
- Geography and incorporation restrictions.
- B2B SaaS, AI, security, enterprise software or Central Asia investment thesis.
- Application form, named contact or portfolio introduction route.
- Why DocFlow's controlled-pilot approach is a specific fit.

## Exclusions

- Do not contact scraped personal emails or send unpersonalised bulk messages.
- Do not apply where the legal entity, geography or stage obviously fails the stated criteria.
- Do not claim Astana Hub residency, users, revenue, customers, patents or partnerships unless verified.
