# DocFlow Pre-Seed Term Discussion Note

This note is an internal discussion aid. It is not a securities offer, legal advice, a term sheet or a commitment to sell equity.

## Founder target

- Capital target: USD 100,000-150,000.
- Indicative dilution: 10-15%.
- Legal form: pre-incorporation; instrument and jurisdiction are undecided.

## External wording

Use: "DocFlow is preparing a USD 100k-150k pre-seed round. We have an indicative 10-15% dilution range and are open to discussing the appropriate instrument and terms after legal formation."

Avoid: publishing an exact valuation, accepting funds, promising ownership, or agreeing to a SAFE, convertible note, shareholder agreement, cap table, discount or valuation cap before founder and legal review.

## Before accepting any investment

1. Incorporate the company and confirm its jurisdiction.
2. Document founder IP assignment and ownership.
3. Prepare a founder-approved cap table.
4. Obtain legal advice on the financing instrument and applicable securities law.
5. Confirm banking, tax, accounting and investor KYC/AML requirements.
