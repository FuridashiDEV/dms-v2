# DocFlow Pre-Seed Brief

## One sentence

DocFlow is a pilot-ready document intelligence product that helps organizations securely store, find, relate and verify business documents without exposing documents outside the user's access rights.

## Problem

Contracts, appendices, acts, invoices and internal decisions are usually fragmented across folders, email and separate systems. Finding the right document is slow; finding the connected document chain is slower; applying AI to this archive is risky if access control and auditability are ignored.

## Product today

DocFlow provides document upload, versions, role and department-based access, organization isolation, audit events, hybrid search, explainable search signals, related-document chains, evidence reports, controlled external document exchange and AI/OCR processing foundations.

The product is ready for investor demonstration and controlled pilots. It is not presented as e-signature software, a certified compliance platform, a fully autonomous AI agent, or a fully managed high-availability deployment.

## Why now

Organizations want the productivity of AI search but cannot accept a system that leaks a confidential contract in a search result. DocFlow's product thesis is that AI document search must be permission-aware, explainable and operationally controllable.

## Initial market focus

The first sales motion is controlled pilots with organizations that handle recurring document chains and need searchable archives: business services, education, healthcare-adjacent administration and quasi-public organizations. The pilot is intentionally narrow: one organization, 2-4 departments, 5-20 users and approved non-sensitive or anonymized documents.

## Raise objective

DocFlow is preparing a USD 100,000-150,000 pre-seed round. The intended use is pilot execution, product reliability work, customer validation and core go-to-market activity. Indicative founder dilution is 10-15%, subject to instrument, valuation, jurisdiction and investor discussion.

## Use of funds framework

- 40%: pilot delivery, customer onboarding and technical reliability.
- 30%: product and search-quality improvement from pilot feedback.
- 20%: sales development, customer discovery and security/pilot documentation.
- 10%: legal formation, accounting and operational reserve.

This is a planning allocation, not a financial forecast.

## Evidence available now

- Demo dataset and a 3-5 minute demo scenario.
- Controlled-pilot deployment checklist and runbook.
- Search-quality benchmark tooling with honest synthetic/semi-real limitations.
- Product, AI/RAG, technical, security and deployment documentation.

## Claims not to make

- No signed customers, revenue, LOIs or paid pilots unless separately verified.
- No SOC 2, ISO or e-signature claim.
- No 99-100% search accuracy claim.
- No guarantee that every AI/OCR operation is automatic, free or error-free.
- No claim that Kubernetes, high availability or a dedicated data centre are production-operational today.
