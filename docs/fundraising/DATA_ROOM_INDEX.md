## Do not share without explicit founder approval

- Source repository access.
- Environment files, passwords, tokens, API keys or private URLs.
- Real customer/pilot documents, PII, file paths, audit payloads or logs.
- Security findings that have not been remediated and contextualized.
- Draft legal documents before legal review.

## Before diligence begins

- Form the legal entity and document IP ownership.
- Prepare a founder-approved cap table.
- Prepare a simple 12-18 month operating model.
- Ensure the email domain, company address and public company information are consistent.
