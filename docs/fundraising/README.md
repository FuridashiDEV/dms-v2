# DocFlow Fundraising Workspace

This folder contains the working material for a controlled pre-seed raise. It is deliberately separate from the product investor pack: the investor pack explains the product, while this folder supports targeting, outreach, founder calls and due diligence.

## Current raise target

- Target amount: USD 100,000-150,000.
- Indicative dilution: 10-15%.
- Stage: pre-seed, pre-incorporation.
- Founder handling investor meetings: Karen Airapetyants.

The target terms are a discussion point, not a public offer or a binding commitment. Any equity instrument, valuation, jurisdiction and closing terms require founder and legal review.

## Files

- `PRE_SEED_BRIEF.md`: concise investment narrative and claim boundaries.
- `PITCH_DECK_OUTLINE.md`: a 10-slide deck script using existing evidence only.
- `INVESTOR_TARGETS.md`: first outreach cohort with public, official routes.
- `OUTREACH_SEQUENCE.md`: transparent email and follow-up messages sent by the DocFlow AI assistant.
- `FOUNDER_CALL_BRIEF.md`: discussion structure and information that must not be improvised.
- `DATA_ROOM_INDEX.md`: materials to share in stages.
- `TERM_DISCUSSION_NOTE.md`: internal valuation/dilution reference and legal guardrails.
- `OUTREACH_TRACKER.csv`: first-cohort follow-up tracker using official public routes.

## Send policy

- Use only public official application or contact routes listed in `INVESTOR_TARGETS.md`.
- Do not claim customers, revenue, certification, legal validity, model accuracy or investment terms that are not confirmed.
- The sender identifies itself as an AI assistant working on behalf of Karen Airapetyants.
- Any discussion of equity, valuation, SAFE/convertible notes, jurisdiction or exclusivity is escalated to Karen Airapetyants.
