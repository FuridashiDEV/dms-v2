# DocFlow Pre-Seed Deck Outline

Use this as a 10-slide narrative. Each slide should remain visual and concise; do not copy the full text onto slides.

## 1. Title

DocFlow — permission-aware document intelligence for controlled enterprise pilots.

Founder: Karen Airapetyants. Stage: pre-incorporation, pre-seed.

## 2. The workflow problem

Show a real-looking but synthetic chain: contract -> appendix -> act -> invoice -> addendum. Explain the time lost when these files live across folders and messages.

## 3. Why generic AI search is insufficient

The answer is not "more AI". It is search that respects document permissions, organization boundaries and audit requirements.

## 4. Product walkthrough

Use the existing demo scenario: upload, search by meaning, see an explanation, open related documents, export an evidence report.

## 5. Core product architecture

Document core, access control, hybrid search, human-confirmed AI suggestions, auditability and controlled external exchange. Keep model/vendor terms out of this slide unless the audience asks.

## 6. Pilot wedge

Controlled first pilot: 1 organization, 2-4 departments, 5-20 users, 100-1,000 approved documents and a 2-4 week duration.

## 7. Market and buyer

Start with document-heavy teams that need faster retrieval without weakening access control. Avoid a top-down TAM claim until supported by a researched market model.

## 8. Why DocFlow can win

Permission-aware search, explainability, related-document chains and evidence-oriented workflows combined in one product. Position this as a product hypothesis validated through pilots, not a proven moat.

## 9. Milestones after investment

Run 2-3 controlled pilots, obtain structured user feedback, establish repeatable onboarding, measure search quality on approved customer data and convert successful pilots into commercial proposals.

## 10. Raise and next step

Seeking USD 100,000-150,000 pre-seed capital. Terms are discussed with the founder. Ask for an introductory call and a review of the controlled-pilot plan.
